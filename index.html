Our Story

Celebrating the Art of Making

Craft Mart was born from a simple belief: that every hand-crafted object holds the spirit of its maker. We set out to build a marketplace that bridges the gap between talented local artisans and people who appreciate the beauty of the hand-made.

From sun-fired clay pots in Jinja to woven mats from Masaka, we are proud to showcase the richness of Ugandan craft tradition — and to help artisans earn a fair, dignified income from their skills.
